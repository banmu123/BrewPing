Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

BrewPing (“the App”) is a developer tool that lets you control AI coding agents running on your own computer from your iPhone and Apple Watch, over your local network. This policy explains what data the App handles — and what it does not.

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.
  • Everything you send stays between your iPhone and your own Mac, on your local network.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BrewPing does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers — we do not operate any servers. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data

2. Permissions and Why We Ask for Them

The App requests only the permissions it needs to function, and each is used solely for the purpose described below:

  • Camera — Used only to scan the pairing QR code shown in the BrewPing desktop app on your Mac. No photos or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — Used only on Apple Watch to turn your spoken words into a text command. Audio is processed on-device by Apple’s speech framework to produce the command text; it is not stored and is not sent to us.
  • Local Network — Used to discover and communicate with the Mac you have paired. The App uses Bonjour (mDNS) to find your Mac, then talks to it directly over your local Wi‑Fi.

3. Where Your Data Goes

Commands you send, the output returned by your coding agent, and any speech that is transcribed travel only between your iPhone (or Apple Watch) and the Mac you configured, directly over your local network. They do not pass through any server operated by us, and we cannot see them.

4. Storage

  • The pairing key that authenticates your iPhone to your Mac is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Device settings (such as your device list and preferences) are stored locally on your device.
  • None of this is stored on our servers, because we do not operate any.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties, because we do not collect any.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at cakbanmu@163.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 11 日

BrewPing（下称“本 App”）是一款开发者工具，让你通过 iPhone 和 Apple Watch，在同一局域网内控制运行在你自己电脑上的 AI 编程助手。本政策说明本 App 会处理哪些数据——以及不会处理哪些数据。

概要

  • 我们不收集任何个人信息。
  • 没有账号、无需注册、无统计分析、无第三方 SDK。
  • 你发送的一切内容，都只在你自己的 iPhone 与你自己的 Mac 之间的局域网内传输。

1. 我们不收集的信息

BrewPing 不会向我们的服务器收集、传输或存储任何个人信息——我们根本没有服务器。具体而言，我们不收集：

  • 姓名、邮箱或联系方式
  • 使用统计或崩溃报告
  • 广告标识符
  • 位置信息

2. 权限及用途

本 App 只申请完成其功能所必需的权限，且每项权限都仅用于以下说明的用途：

  • 相机 —— 仅用于扫描 Mac 上 BrewPing 桌面端显示的配对二维码。不录制、不存储、不传输任何照片或视频。
  • 麦克风与语音识别 —— 仅用于在 Apple Watch 上把你的语音转成文字指令。音频由 Apple 的语音框架在设备本地处理以生成指令文字，我们不会存储，也不会发送给我们。
  • 本地网络 —— 用于发现并与你已配对的 Mac 通信。本 App 通过 Bonjour（mDNS）找到你的 Mac，然后直接在同一 Wi‑Fi 内与它通信。

3. 数据流向

你发送的指令、编程助手返回的输出、以及被转写的语音，只在你自己的 iPhone（或 Apple Watch）与你配置的 Mac 之间，经局域网直接传输。它们不会经过我们运营的任何服务器，我们也看不到。

4. 存储

  • 用于验证 iPhone 身份的配对密钥，存放在你设备上的 iOS 钥匙串（Keychain） 中。
  • 设备设置（如设备列表与偏好）存放在你设备本地。
  • 我们不会把这些存到我们服务器上，因为我们没有服务器。

5. 第三方共享

我们不向任何第三方共享、出售或转让任何数据，因为我们不收集任何数据。

6. 儿童隐私

本 App 是开发者工具，并非面向儿童。我们不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

7. 政策变更

若本政策有变更，我们会更新页面顶部的“最后更新”日期。

8. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请发邮件至 cakbanmu@163.com。
